## SKIP / IGNORE RULES

Do NOT create transactions for these — they are internal transfers, not real income/expenses:
- "Money added via Apple Pay" or similar Apple Pay top-ups
- Card top-ups, account top-ups
- Transfers between own accounts

If a transaction matches a skip rule, omit it entirely from the output.

---

## CONFIDENCE RULES

- 0.90–1.00: Amount and merchant clearly identifiable, known category mapping
- 0.70–0.89: Amount clear but merchant/category inferred
- 0.50–0.69: Partially obscured or ambiguous, fallback category used
- 0.00–0.49: Cannot reliably identify the transaction
